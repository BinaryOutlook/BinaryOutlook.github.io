01 / About

Curious systems, real constraints.

I like ideas that survive contact with code: clear workflows, measurable models, fast kernels, and interfaces people can use.

Technology moves quickly, and my interests often move with it. What stays constant is the drive to explore deeply, learn with intent, and understand how technical progress becomes something genuinely useful for people.

I am most interested in work that carries a strong technical idea the whole distance from concept to product, tool, or research direction. If your interests overlap, I would like to hear from you.
